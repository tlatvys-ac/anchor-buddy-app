ANCHOR BUDDY

A motion designer's utility panel for After Effects — anchor points, alignment, colors, easing curves, layer tools, and text rigs, in one panel.

Download Anchor Buddy (.zxp) After Effects extension · free · macOS & Windows

What's inside

  • AnchorOne-click anchor placement (mask-aware), align & distribute, comp guides.
  • ColorsA color library, custom palettes, and fill/stroke editing: eyedropper, on/off, one-click swap.
  • CurvesCopy, paste, and preset easing curves between keyframes.
  • LayersStagger, reverse order, batch rename, null tools.
  • TypeCounters, text splitting, box rigs, wave and cascade animators.
  • ShapesUI builder rigs, corner control, vector rebuilds, liner.
  • AnimationPosterizer, randomizer, motion time, point-at rigs, blob noise.
  • CustomCollect your favorite tools into one tab, in your order.

Setup

  1. Install the extension. Open the .zxp with a ZXP installer (aescripts ZXP Installer is free).
  2. Restart After Effects. Then open Window → Extensions → Anchor Buddy.

"Unverified publisher"?

The extension is signed with a self-signed certificate — your ZXP installer may show an "unverified publisher" notice. That's expected.

Requirements

After Effects 2020 or later (developed and tested on AE 2026) · macOS and Windows. Everything runs locally in the panel — no accounts, no network calls.
